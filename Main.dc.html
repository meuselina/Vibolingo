The problem

Everyone can generate code now.
Almost no one can direct it.

Millions of people are "vibe coding" — describing an app to an AI and shipping whatever it hands back. Most do it badly: they can't tell good output from bad, don't know how to scope a prompt, and freeze the first time the AI confidently breaks something. Traditional coding courses don't help — they teach syntax, for a world where you typed every line yourself.

The scarce skill flipped. It's no longer writing code — it's directing AI to write good code. Nobody is teaching that as a daily habit yet.

The solution

Vibolingo borrows Duolingo's habit engine — and points it at a skill with a real payoff.

A skill path, bite-size lessons, streaks and leagues — the loop that made a daily habit out of language learning for hundreds of millions of people. Except every Vibolingo lesson ends with something Duolingo never gives you: a real, live, shipped piece of software.

Skill Path

A winding map of units — Your First App, Prompting Like a Pro, Debug With AI, Ship It — unlocked one node at a time.

The Lesson

Prompt an AI in a chat pane on the left, watch a real app render live on the right. Lose a heart when a prompt ships something broken.

Vibe Check

Spot-the-bug drills — a leaked key, an infinite loop, a confusing screen. Catch it before you'd ship it: the grammar drill of vibe coding.

Streaks & Leagues

A daily streak flame, weekly leagues, promotion and demotion. The addictive machinery — borrowed on purpose.

Shipped Portfolio

Every lesson's output is a tiny real app, saved to your public profile. Your streak isn't a badge — it's a portfolio.
